# Arrange a meeting with fastn

Use only when the user asked for a person, or a real attempt failed.

1. Write the handover (keep it under 15 lines):
   - Company and product, in one line.
   - Requirement summary, as confirmed.
   - Verdict and the solution table.
   - Gaps and open questions.
   - What was already tried.
2. Show it to the user and ask them to correct it.
3. Tell the user to open **Help (?) → Contact support** in the fastn dashboard, ask for a call, and paste the handover, so the call starts where the chat ended. Do not collect their name, email or times in the chat.

Do not promise a response time. Do not collect passwords, keys or customer data for the meeting.
